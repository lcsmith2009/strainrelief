Education Center

Simple cannabis wellness education written with safer, non-medical wording.

🌿 THC vs CBD

THC is commonly associated with intoxicating effects, while CBD-forward options may feel less intense for some users. Products vary widely, so ask for lab-tested information and start low.

🥭 What is Myrcene?

Myrcene is a terpene often associated with earthy aroma, body relaxation, and nighttime-style directions. It does not guarantee a specific effect.

🐢 Start Low and Go Slow

Newer users may want to begin with lower THC, balanced THC/CBD, or CBD-forward options. Give products time to take effect and avoid stacking too much too quickly.

🌙 Daytime vs Nighttime

Daytime directions are often described as brighter, clearer, or more functional. Nighttime directions are often associated with heavier relaxation or sleepiness.

🛒 Dispensary Questions

Ask about THC percentage, CBD content, terpene profile, serving size, onset time, lab testing, and beginner-friendly options. Follow local laws and do not drive while impaired.

📝 Why Journaling Helps

Tracking strain, serving size, time of day, and how you felt can help you remember what directions felt comfortable or too intense.
